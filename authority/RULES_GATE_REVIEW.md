# Rules gate review — frozen 0.1.0
3 October 2026

## Frozen decisions
| ID | Authoritative rule | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | Highest initiative D6 becomes attacker; ties reroll both. | User explicitly authorized this departure from the PDF initiative-choice procedure. |
| D2 | On exhausted pools with both alive, lower percentage of own starting Wounds lost wins. Attacker wins only exact equality. | Compare integer cross-products; do not use raw damage or rounded display percentages. No additional pool or draw. |
| D3 | Initial roll → optional weapon rerolls → optional Command choice → final retention/Rending → Strike/Block. | Each die may be rerolled only once across all reroll sources. |
| Command cap | At most one use per player per bout, at most two per player per match. | One token rerolls one eligible die; free weapon rerolls remain separate. |

These user decisions supersede the earlier review proposal and any conflicting sequencing in the kickoff package. No unresolved rule-authority conflict remains. Percentage-loss scoring is equivalently a comparison of the percentage of enemy starting Wounds removed: each loss is divided by the affected fighter's own starting Wounds.

## Checks completed

## O.O.A. assessment
The bounded all-Strike screening does not show a roster-wide inability to produce O.O.A. outcomes. Slagjaw mirrors show a low-end frequency signal worth monitoring. This is not optimal-play evidence and does not authorize rebalancing; no roster change was made. Full balance and human timing tests remain later-stage work.

## Gate scope
READY means the rules are consistent and sufficiently specified to proceed to Visual Identity. It does not mean an implemented game has passed QA or that its pacing, balance, artwork, hosting or competition submission is validated. No further Stage 1 decisions are unresolved.
